This page sets out TweeTok's standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). TweeTok is a third-party viewer, published on Google Play by the developer yuanming.

Zero tolerance: TweeTok strictly prohibits all child sexual abuse and exploitation content and conduct, including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Prohibited content and conduct

When using TweeTok, the following are strictly prohibited:

  • Posting, sharing, soliciting or promoting child sexual abuse material;
  • Grooming, sextorting or otherwise sexually exploiting a minor;
  • Sexualizing minors, or promoting or glorifying child sexual abuse and exploitation;
  • Facilitating the trafficking or sexual exploitation of minors.

2. Where content on TweeTok comes from

TweeTok does not host user content itself. Posts, images and videos in the app are hosted by X and are subject to X's rules. The app is intended for users aged 18 and over.

3. How to report

  • In the app: tap "More" (⋯) on the post, choose "Report or Block" → "Report Post", and select "Child safety" as the reason. The post is removed from your feed immediately and we receive the report.
  • On X: please also report it to X. Only X can remove content from its platform or suspend accounts.
  • By email: wym572@gmail.com. Please do not attach any images or videos involved. A link to the post is enough.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police right away. You can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline or the relevant authority in your region.

4. What we do

  • We review reports related to child safety;
  • We report the content and accounts involved to X;
  • Where child sexual abuse material is confirmed, we report it to NCMEC or the relevant regional or national authority;
  • We comply with applicable child safety laws and with lawful requests from the authorities.

5. Child safety contact

For questions about these standards or child safety, contact: wym572@gmail.com
